For my personal vault I use iCloud sync (which is free) to keep my vault synced across my Apple devices.

> [!tip] Hyphens in note names has caused me iCloud syncing issues previously

I also have use [this community plugin](https://github.com/velviagris/obsidian-local-backup) to generate a backup each time the app launches on my Mac.

I keep my work vault complete separate and use whichever cloud drive service is used by the company, as well as local git versioning instead of the community backup plugin.
