# Why use Obsidian?

I've gone all-in in Obsidian in my personal and professional lives to take notes about (and therefore not have to remember) everything.

I've found using Obsidian in the way I will describe has removed those nagging thoughts that I'm forgetting something, or the feeling of being overwhelmed by the volume of information I need to keep track of. I can just trust in my information-processing workflow that I can access what I need when I need it.

This topic is known as *Personal Knowledge Management* or PKM.

The main principles are about
- minimising the friction to capturing information 
- very fast access and navigation of information
- linking thoughts together to create an information network over time that provides value

This frees your brain capacity up from being used for storage and allows you to focus on information processing. I personally find this reduces stress and makes life easier.
